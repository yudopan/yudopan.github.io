Yuandong Pan

Postdoctoral Researcher, School of Engineering, Stanford University

I am a civil engineering researcher developing dependable AI for the digital transformation and lifecycle management of civil infrastructure. My work links large language models and vision-language models with geometry, asset relationships, and source evidence, so engineers can make verifiable decisions about buildings, roads, and other assets across their whole lifecycle.

    About

    I am a postdoctoral researcher in the School of Engineering at Stanford University, working with Prof. Michael Lepech. Before Stanford, I was a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Actions Future Road Research Fellow at the University of Cambridge with Prof. Ioannis Brilakis, and a Visiting Fellow at the University of Hong Kong. I completed my PhD at the Technical University of Munich, co-advised by Prof. André Borrmann and Prof. Ioannis Brilakis, on creating information-rich digital twins of indoor environments from image and point-cloud data.

    My work takes a systems-and-deployment perspective: making learning methods reliable, efficient, and trustworthy in the real world, not only in principle. It has been recognised with prizes in the CVPR Scan-to-BIM competitions and a journal Best Paper Award, and supported by competitive fellowships and funding as principal investigator. I am also interested in translating research into practice: I completed the Stanford Ignite programme at the Graduate School of Business and co-founded a startup that uses LLMs to support logistics optimization.
